Laboratory Microscopes

High-precision microscopes for clinical diagnostics, research, and educational applications.

Detailed Description

Professional-grade microscopes with superior optics and ergonomic design. Suitable for various applications including pathology, hematology, and microbiology with multiple magnification options and digital imaging capabilities. Features include LED illumination, mechanical stage, and precision focusing system.

Specifications

  • Magnification: 40x to 1000x
  • Illumination: LED
  • Camera: Optional digital attachment
  • Objectives: Achromatic or plan objectives
  • Eyepieces: WF10x or WF15x
