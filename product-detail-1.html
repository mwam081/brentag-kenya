Chemistry Analyzers

Advanced automated systems for clinical chemistry testing with high precision and reliability.

Detailed Description

Fully automated chemistry analyzers designed for high-volume clinical laboratories. These systems provide accurate and reliable results for a wide range of chemistry tests including glucose, cholesterol, electrolytes, enzymes, and proteins. Features include random access testing, STAT capability, and comprehensive quality control.

Specifications

  • Throughput: Up to 400 tests/hour
  • Test Menu: 50+ parameters
  • Sample Volume: 2-35µL
  • Reagent Capacity: 40+ reagents onboard
  • Calibration: Automatic multi-point calibration
