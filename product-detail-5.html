Laboratory Centrifuges

High-speed centrifuges for various laboratory applications including blood separation.

Detailed Description

Reliable laboratory centrifuges designed for various applications including clinical diagnostics, research, and blood banking. Features include programmable operation, digital display, safety lid lock, and imbalance detection. Available in different capacities and rotor configurations to suit various laboratory needs.

Specifications

  • Max Speed: 6000 rpm
  • Max RCF: 5000 x g
  • Capacity: 4x100mL or 24x15mL
  • Timer: 1-99 minutes
  • Safety: Imbalance detection, lid lock

Key Features

Digital speed control

Programmable timer

Safety lid lock

Imbalance detection

Multiple rotor options

Quiet operation
