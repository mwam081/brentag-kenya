X-Ray Machines

Digital X-ray systems for high-quality diagnostic imaging with advanced features.

Detailed Description

Advanced digital X-ray systems designed for medical diagnostic imaging. These systems provide high-resolution images with low radiation exposure, featuring digital radiography technology, automated positioning, and comprehensive imaging software for accurate diagnosis across various medical applications.

Specifications

  • Technology: Digital Radiography (DR)
  • Power: 50-100 kW generator
  • Tube Voltage: 40-150 kV
  • Image Resolution: Up to 12 megapixels
  • Exposure Time: 0.001-5.0 seconds

Key Features

High-frequency generator

Digital image processing

Automatic exposure control

Radiation safety features

DICOM compatibility

Portable options available

Applications

Chest Radiography Orthopedic Imaging Dental Imaging Emergency Room Pediatric Imaging

Safety Features

  • Automatic collimation
  • Radiation dose monitoring
  • Overload protection
  • Thermal protection system
