Patient Monitors

Advanced patient monitoring systems for critical care and general ward applications.

Detailed Description

Multi-parameter patient monitors with high-resolution displays and intuitive interfaces. Monitor vital signs including ECG, SpO2, NIBP, temperature and more with customizable alarm settings and data trending. Designed for reliability and ease of use in various clinical settings.

Specifications

  • Parameters: ECG, SpO2, NIBP, Temp
  • Display: 12.1" TFT LCD
  • Battery: 4+ hours backup
  • Connectivity: Ethernet, USB
  • Alarms: Visual and audible
